Introduction

Prakarsa (PT Prakarsa Mahakarya Abadi) is an engineering firm based in Yogyakarta, Indonesia that provides civil engineering consulting and construction services.

Initially founded as CERTC (Civil Engineering Research and Training Center) for developing skills of civil engineering students in Yogyakarta under the guidance of Joko Sumiyanto, CERTC has grown into a capable and highly-competent engineering firm. Over the past 10 years, we have executed numerous projects ranging from buildings, bridges, roads, docks, and other civil structures.

As of 2021, CERTC has been legalized (AHU-0041435.AH.01.01.Tahun 2021) and rebranded as Prakarsa.
